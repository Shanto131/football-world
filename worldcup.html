FIFA World Cup

The FIFA World Cup is one of the biggest football tournaments in the world. National teams from different countries compete to become world champions. The men's World Cup is normally held every four years.

Basic Information

  • First men's tournament: 1930
  • Organized by: FIFA
  • Held every four years
  • Teams represent their countries
  • The winner becomes world champion

World Cup History

The first men's FIFA World Cup took place in Uruguay in 1930. Uruguay won the first tournament. Since then, the competition has become a major event watched by football fans around the world.

Important Facts

  1. Uruguay won the first men's World Cup in 1930.
  2. Brazil has won the men's tournament five times.

How Does It Work?

National teams qualify for the tournament through regional competitions. The qualified teams then compete in the World Cup finals.

  • Qualification: Teams compete to earn a place.
  • Group stage: Teams play matches in groups.
  • Knockout stage: Teams must win to advance.
  • Final: Two teams compete for the trophy.
