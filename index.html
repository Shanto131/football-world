Welcome to Football World

Football is one of the most popular sports in the world. Two teams play against each other, and each team tries to score goals by putting the ball into the opponent's net. Football brings people together through teamwork, passion, and competition.

Basic Football Information

  • Each team usually has 11 players on the field.
  • A standard match lasts 90 minutes, divided into two halves.
  • The goalkeeper protects the goal.
  • Players mainly use their feet to control the ball.
  • The team scoring more goals wins the match.

FIFA World Cup

The FIFA World Cup is a major international football tournament. National teams compete to become world champions. It is normally held every four years.

World Cup Facts

  1. The first men's FIFA World Cup took place in 1930.
  2. Brazil has won the men's tournament five times.
  3. The tournament brings together national teams from around the world.
